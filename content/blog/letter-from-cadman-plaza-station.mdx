*This article is general information, not legal advice. Deadlines and procedures can change, so confirm the details with the NYC Department of Finance before you act.*

## What is Cadman Plaza Station on a parking ticket letter?

This address is the Department of Finance's hearing-by-mail address for parking ticket disputes. The full address is [Hearings By Mail Unit, P.O. Box 29021, Cadman Plaza Station, Brooklyn, NY 11202-9021](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-mail.page), addressed to the NYC Department of Finance.

## Where exactly do I address my dispute letter?

Address it to the NYC Department of Finance, Hearings By Mail Unit, exactly as the Department publishes the address on its [hearing-by-mail page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-mail.page): P.O. Box 29021, Cadman Plaza Station, Brooklyn, NY 11202-9021.

As a general USPS rule, when mail shows both a street address and a P.O. box, the Postal Service [delivers to the line immediately above the city and state](https://pe.usps.com/cpim/ftp/manuals/dmm300/602.pdf). Copy the address as the Department of Finance publishes it, and check that page for current instructions on what to include with your request.

## How long do I have to dispute by mail?

You must request a hearing within 30 days after the ticket was issued. The [Department of Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page) says this is the window to avoid late penalties, and [NYC311](https://on.nyc.gov/ParkingViolationDispute) states that a mailed request must reach the Department of Finance within 30 days of the violation date.

Notice the wording: the request must *reach* the Department. The sources reviewed for this article do not say whether a postmark or proof of mailing counts as the date of receipt, so do not assume it does. Mail early and keep copies.

Also note that [contacting anyone other than the Department of Finance does not pause the 30-day deadline](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page).

## What happens if I miss the 30-day deadline?

Two separate things can happen. First, according to [NYC311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02348), a $25 penalty is added to the fine after 30 days. Second, if the Department of Finance receives [neither payment nor a hearing request, the ticket goes into judgment about 100 days after issuance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page). A ticket in judgment that is more than one year old cannot get a hearing.

The same 30-day deadline applies if you mistakenly paid a ticket but still want to dispute it. According to [NYC311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02348), that dispute must be made by mail or in person.

## Do I have to send a letter at all?

No. The [Department of Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page) lets you dispute online, by mail, or through the Pay or Dispute mobile app, and in-person hearings are available if you can't use those options.

For a mailed hearing, [the decision is emailed to the address you supplied](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page), so use an email address you check regularly.

## What if the judge finds me guilty?

You can appeal. If a judge finds you guilty, you can file a Parking/Camera Violation Appeal Application [within 30 days of the hearing decision](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket-faqs.page). Calendar that date as soon as you receive the decision.

## Where do I find the current instructions for a mailed request?

The Department of Finance publishes the current requirements on its [hearing-by-mail page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-mail.page), so follow those instructions rather than relying on a template from elsewhere. Need to write one? [Start a letter](/letters).

## FAQ

**What is the mailing address for a NYC parking ticket dispute?**
NYC Department of Finance, Hearings By Mail Unit, P.O. Box 29021, Cadman Plaza Station, Brooklyn, NY 11202-9021, per the [Department of Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-mail.page).

**How long do I have to dispute a NYC parking ticket by mail?**
You must request a hearing within 30 days after the ticket was issued, and the request must reach the Department of Finance within that window, per [NYC311](https://on.nyc.gov/ParkingViolationDispute).

**Do I have to dispute by mail?**
No. Online, mail, the Pay or Dispute app, and in-person hearings are all options, per the [Department of Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page).

**What happens if I miss the deadline?**
A $25 penalty is added to the fine after 30 days per [NYC311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02348), and a ticket with no payment or hearing request goes into judgment about 100 days after issuance per the [Department of Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page).

**What if the judge finds me guilty?**
File a Parking/Camera Violation Appeal Application within 30 days of the hearing decision, per the [Department of Finance FAQ](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket-faqs.page).
