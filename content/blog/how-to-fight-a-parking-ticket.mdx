*This article is general information, not legal advice. Parking rules and deadlines are set locally and can change, so confirm the details with the agency that issued your ticket.*

## How long do I have to fight a parking ticket?

It depends on the city. Deadlines differ from place to place, from 7 days in Chicago to 30 days in NYC and DC. This article covers only four jurisdictions, so do not assume yours matches any of them.

- **New York City:** you must [request a hearing within 30 days of the ticket's issue date](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page) to avoid late penalties if you lose.
- **California:** you have [21 calendar days from issuance (or 14 days from the mailing of a notice of delinquent parking violation)](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf) to request a free initial review under Cal. Vehicle Code § 40215(a), as reproduced by the Orange County Superior Court.
- **Washington, DC:** [contesting within 30 calendar days](https://dmv.dc.gov/service/contest-parking-and-photo-enforcement-tickets-05) means only the original fine applies if you are found responsible.
- **Chicago:** the registered owner has [seven days from issuance to contest a ticket](https://parkingtickets.chicago.gov/EHearingWeb/FrequentlyAskedQuestions).

Start with the instructions printed on your own ticket and the issuing agency's website.

## What is the first step to contest a ticket?

Find out how your issuing agency accepts disputes, and act before the deadline. In NYC, [contacting anyone other than the Department of Finance does not pause the deadline](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page).

NYC tickets can be [disputed online, by mail, or through the Pay or Dispute mobile app](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page), with an in-person hearing available if you can't use those options. Chicago contests can be [filed by correspondence, in person, or virtually](https://parkingtickets.chicago.gov/EHearingWeb/FrequentlyAskedQuestions).

Whatever the method, write down the ticket number, the date, and the deadline before doing anything else.

## How does California's three-step process work?

California uses a staged process under Cal. Vehicle Code § 40215, as reproduced in an [Orange County Superior Court information sheet](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf):

1. **Initial review.** Request a free review within the 21-day (or 14-day) window above.
2. **Administrative hearing.** If the review goes against you, you must request a hearing within 21 calendar days of the mailing of the review results.
3. **Court appeal.** After an adverse hearing decision, you have 30 calendar days from mailing or personal delivery to appeal to Superior Court under § 40230(a).

Two details matter. Under § 40215(b), you must [deposit the parking penalty to request an administrative hearing](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf), and the City of Long Beach confirms this [can be waived for inability to pay](https://www.longbeach.gov/parking/citations/). And the hearing must be held [within 90 calendar days of your request, with one continuance of up to 21 calendar days available](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf).

## How do I contest a ticket by mail?

Follow the issuing agency's mail instructions closely. In Chicago, a contest by mail must be [a signed statement from the registered owner with supporting evidence, with the ticket number on all documents](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html).

Keep copies of everything you send. In Chicago, [the hearing officer's decision is final](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html), so submit your strongest case the first time.

## What happens if I miss the deadline?

Penalties and lost rights add up quickly. In NYC, [a $25 penalty is added after 30 days](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02348). An unpaid, undisputed ticket [goes into judgment about 100 days after issuance, and a ticket in judgment more than one year old cannot get a hearing](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page).

In DC, the timing changes the cost: [contesting between days 31 and 60 means paying both the fine and a penalty](https://dmv.dc.gov/service/contest-parking-and-photo-enforcement-tickets-05) if you are found responsible. If the ticket is more than 60 days old, DC allows a Motion to Vacate, which [DC DMV must receive within 120 calendar days of the issue date](https://dmv.dc.gov/service/contest-parking-and-photo-enforcement-tickets-05). DC DMV's pages give different windows for different steps, so read the page for your specific situation.

## What if I lose the hearing?

You may be able to appeal, but the deadline is short. In NYC, you can file a [Parking/Camera Violation Appeal Application within 30 days of the hearing decision](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket-faqs.page). In California, you can [appeal to Superior Court within 30 calendar days](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf) under § 40230(a). In Chicago, a mail-contest decision is [final](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html).

## Put your dispute in writing

If you contest by mail in Chicago, the city requires [a signed statement from the registered owner with supporting evidence and the ticket number on all documents](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html). In NYC, mail is one of the [available ways to dispute a ticket](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page). If you want help drafting a clear written statement, you can [build a dispute letter here](/letters).

## FAQ

**Is there one national process for fighting a parking ticket?**
No. Parking tickets are handled by the city or agency that issued them, so deadlines, steps, and penalties differ. This article covers only New York City, California, Washington DC, and Chicago. Check the issuing agency's instructions on your ticket, such as the [NYC Department of Finance dispute page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page).

**What happens if I miss the deadline?**
It depends on the city. In NYC, a $25 penalty is added after 30 days, per [NYC311](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02348), and an undisputed ticket goes into judgment about 100 days after issuance, [per NYC Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page). In DC, contesting between days 31 and 60 means paying the fine plus a penalty if you are found responsible, [per DC DMV](https://dmv.dc.gov/service/contest-parking-and-photo-enforcement-tickets-05).

**Can I contest a ticket by mail instead of in person?**
In several cities, yes. [NYC allows online, mail, and mobile app disputes](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket.page), and Chicago accepts [contests by correspondence, in person, or virtually](https://parkingtickets.chicago.gov/EHearingWeb/FrequentlyAskedQuestions). A Chicago mail contest requires [a signed statement from the registered owner with supporting evidence](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html).

**Can I appeal if I lose the hearing?**
In NYC, yes, within 30 days of the decision, [per NYC Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-a-ticket-faqs.page). In California, you can appeal to Superior Court within 30 days under [Cal. Vehicle Code § 40230(a)](https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/l1179.pdf). In Chicago, [the hearing officer's decision on a mail contest is final](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/parking_and_red-lightnoticeinformation5/contest_by_mail.html).

**Can I still dispute an old ticket?**
Sometimes. DC allows a Motion to Vacate for tickets older than 60 days, but [DC DMV must receive it within 120 days of the issue date](https://dmv.dc.gov/service/contest-parking-and-photo-enforcement-tickets-05). In NYC, a ticket in judgment for more than a year cannot get a hearing, [per NYC Finance](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/vehicles/dispute-web.page).
