*This article is general information, not legal advice. Deadlines depend on the agency and the specific notice you received, so read your notice carefully and confirm details with the issuing agency or a qualified professional.*

## How long do I have to correct a notice of violation?

It depends on the agency and the notice, and the deadline written in your notice controls. There is no universal federal correction period. Examples show how much it varies: under the Interior Department's offshore rules, [the matter is closed if you correct all violations in a Notice of Noncompliance within 20 days of receiving it, or within a longer period the Notice specifies](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/chapter-II/subchapter-B/part-250/subpart-N/subject-group-ECFR0b8f3177bf357a1/section-250.1452). For HUD multifamily properties, [a Notice of Violation/Default carries a cure period (for example, 60 days), and HUD schedules re-inspection after the cure period expires, including any approved extension](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/15-02hsgn.pdf).

Even the term itself varies by place. [New Jersey's rules, published on EPA's site, give a 30-day grace period for minor violations that meet set conditions, while non-minor violations get no grace period](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2018-07/documents/7.27a_-3.10_oct_2016_eff_date.pdf).

## What is the deadline to contest an OSHA citation versus correct it?

The contest deadline is 15 working days from receipt; correction is due by the abatement date on the citation. [A notice of intention to contest must be postmarked within 15 working days of the employer's receipt of the notice of proposed penalty](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.17). In counting, [the day of receipt is not counted and the last day is counted](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XVII/part-1903).

Missing that window matters. [If you don't contest within 15 working days, the citation becomes a final order not subject to review by any court or agency](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3000.pdf). Treat the two clocks separately: one for deciding whether to contest, one for fixing the hazard.

## What proof of correction do I need to send OSHA?

You must certify abatement in writing. [Within 10 calendar days after the abatement date, the employer must certify to OSHA that each cited violation has been abated](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.19). That certification [must include the date and method of abatement and a statement that affected employees and their representatives were informed](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.19).

Two related points from [OSHA's abatement guidance](https://www.osha.gov/abatement): the citation must remain posted until the violation is abated or for 3 working days, whichever is longer, and if the citation notes that a violation was corrected during the inspection, no abatement certification is required for that item.

## What proof do I need for an EPA notice of violation?

It depends on the program, and this article did not find one rule covering every EPA notice. One documented example is EPA's Audit Policy for self-disclosed violations. Under it, [a regulated entity must correct the violation within 60 days and certify correction in writing, and if more time is needed it must notify EPA in writing before the 60 days pass](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2000/04/11/00-8954/incentives-for-self-policing-discovery-disclosure-correction-and-prevention-of-violations).

That policy governs penalty mitigation for self-disclosure. It is a 2000 policy statement whose current status was not checked, and it should not be read as the correction rule for every EPA notice. Follow the instructions in the notice you actually received.

## What happens if I ignore an EPA notice of violation?

EPA can escalate. Under the Clean Air Act, [EPA may issue an order or bring a civil action at any time after 30 days following the date a notice of violation is issued](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/pdf/USCODE-2023-title42-chap85-subchapI-partA-sec7413.pdf). The 30-day mark is when those enforcement options open up, not a deadline by which you must act.

## Will correcting the violation remove the penalty?

This article found no agency-wide rule saying that correction eliminates penalties, so do not assume it does. Check how your own notice treats penalties. Two sourced examples show the range. Under the Interior offshore rule, [the matter is closed if you correct all violations in a Notice of Noncompliance within 20 days of receiving it, or within a longer period the Notice specifies](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/chapter-II/subchapter-B/part-250/subpart-N/subject-group-ECFR0b8f3177bf357a1/section-250.1452). Under OSHA, [the 15-working-day contest period runs from receipt of the notice of proposed penalty](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.17).

## How do I respond in writing?

Keep a record of the date you received the notice, your corrective steps and their dates, and any photos or invoices. Then send whatever the notice requires. For OSHA, that means a certification stating [the date and method of abatement and that affected employees and their representatives were informed](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.19), submitted within 10 calendar days after the abatement date. If you need to write to an agency to confirm correction or ask a question, reference the notice number and the deadline in your notice. You can [draft a letter here](/letters).

## FAQ

**Is there one federal deadline for correcting a notice of violation?** No. This article found no single federal rule. Each agency, program, and notice sets its own period, so the deadline written in your notice controls.

**What are the OSHA deadlines to contest a citation and to correct the violation?** The contest window is [15 working days from receipt of the notice of proposed penalty](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.17). Abatement is due by the abatement date on the citation, and the [abatement certification is due within 10 calendar days after that date](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.19).

**What do I send to prove an OSHA violation was corrected?** A certification showing the date and method of abatement and a statement that affected employees and their representatives were informed, due within 10 calendar days after the abatement date, per [29 CFR 1903.19](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1903/1903.19).

**What happens if I ignore an EPA notice of violation?** Under [Clean Air Act section 113(a)(1)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/pdf/USCODE-2023-title42-chap85-subchapI-partA-sec7413.pdf), EPA may issue an order or bring a civil action at any time after 30 days following the date the notice is issued.

**Do state or local notices of violation follow the same deadlines?** Not necessarily. State and local rules differ, and this article did not verify them. Read your notice and check with the issuing agency.
